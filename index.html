    Accurate answers

    Three ways to get accurate answers.

    01

    Incentives

    A blank is better than a guess.

    Tell it a wrong answer is 3× worse than a blank.

    02

    Sourcing

    Only use my files.

    Not from its brain. Not from the internet.

    03

    Citations

    Show me where.

    The exact words, and where they are.

    Accurate answers

    Add these three lines to any task that uses documents.

    Copy and pasteAccuracy block
    SourcingBase every answer only on the files I've provided. Nothing else.
    IncentivesA wrong answer is 3x worse than a blank. If it isn't there, write NOT FOUND and say why.
    CitationsFor every answer, quote the exact words and say where they are. If it helps, link to that section.

    Ask what it decided for you.
